# Outpatient Queue Management System (OQMS)

**Domain:** Healthcare | **Type:** Business Analyst Case Study

## Overview
A hospital's outpatient department lacked visibility and structure in its queue 
process, with patients waiting 3–4 hours past appointment times and no protection 
against publicly exposing patient information. This case study designs a 
privacy first, offline resilient queue and appointment system across 9 stakeholder 
groups, with a deliberately scoped boundary against building a full medical 
records system.

## Key Artifacts
- Stakeholder Analysis 
- Business Requirements Document (BRD), including two explicit NFRs.
- As-Is/To-Be Gap Analysis
- Process Flow Diagram
- User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

## Skills Demonstrated
Non functional requirements engineering (privacy, reliability), deliberate scope 
boundary setting, dependency/risk identification, stakeholder analysis across a 
complex multi department environment, process flow diagramming.
